# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on code, design, evidence, and outcomes.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact options. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of the reporter.

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior considered inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
